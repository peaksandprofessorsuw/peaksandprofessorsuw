Events

Hikes, Peaks x Plungers, thrift pop-ups, workshops, and collabs with other clubs. Everything we have coming up is on this calendar.

The Google Calendar will appear here. Add the club calendar's ID in js/config.js to turn it on. In the meantime, upcoming events are posted on Instagram.

Click any event to see details and its sign-up link. Want to hear about new events first? Join the mailing list.

How to sign up

  1. Find a hike or event you're excited about, on this calendar, in our emails, or on Instagram.

  2. Open the sign-up link and fill out the Google Form. If the form is closed, the trip and its waitlist are full.

  3. Within a few days your trip lead will email you. Pay the small gas fee and reply within 24 hours to lock in your spot. If you're waitlisted, hang tight: we often get through a good part of the list.

  4. A few days before, you'll get a reminder with the meeting time, location, and what to expect.

  5. Show up ready to get outside and have great conversations with students and professors.
