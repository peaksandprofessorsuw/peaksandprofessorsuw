Our Team

Trip leads are the backbone of Peaks. They reach out to professors, plan every trip, and lead them on the trail.

Join the team

Trip lead applications open once a year during fall quarter. Trip leads commit to a weekly meeting, leading at least two hikes a quarter, and a quarterly retreat.
