Think Outside

Peaks & Professors brings UW students and professors together on hikes and outdoor adventures across Western Washington, with good conversation the whole way up.

See upcoming events Join the mailing list Follow @peaksandprofessorsuw

Get outside with your professors

No membership, no meetings. Find a hike or event that sounds fun, sign up, and show up.

Every trip includes a UW professor who shares a short talk, a topic they love, or a Q&A on the trail. We handle the planning and carpools, so you don't need a car or any experience.

New hikes are announced on the mailing list and Instagram first.
