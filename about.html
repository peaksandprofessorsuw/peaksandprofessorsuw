About

A registered student organization at the University of Washington, uniting students and professors through the outdoors.

Our mission

Peaks & Professors creates meaningful experiences that spark unique conversations between students, faculty, and the great outdoors.

By giving people a way to Think Outside, we help students and professors build real relationships through curious conversations in beautiful places. We love collaborating with other UW student organizations and departments, and we hope to see you on the trail.

What we do

Hiking is at our core: day hikes all over Western Washington, each one joined by a professor who brings a talk, a topic, or a Q&A to the trail. Our professors come from every corner of campus, from Mechanical Engineering to Ancient Asian Languages to Botany.

We also run community events throughout the year, like Peaks x Plungers, thrift pop-ups, interactive workshops with faculty, and collaborations with other clubs. Keep an eye on the Events calendar and Instagram for what's next.
